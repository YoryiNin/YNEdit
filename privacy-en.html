YNEdit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

YNEdit is a video-editing application by YNStudios. This policy explains what information the app may process and the third-party services it uses.

1. Content You Edit

Videos, images, audio, recordings, text, and projects that you choose to edit are processed locally on your device to provide editing and export features. The app stores projects and required temporary files on your device and saves exported videos to the location you choose. YNEdit does not upload your project contents or media files to YNStudios servers.

The app may request access to your camera, microphone, or device media when you use features that need them. You choose which files to import and where to save an export. Exported files saved to a shared location remain there until you delete them.

2. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

YNEdit includes banner and rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads. After creating four projects, you can watch a rewarded ad to unlock each additional project; an active Premium subscription removes ads and this limit. The app requests advertising consent information through Google User Messaging Platform before initializing Google Mobile Ads and, where required by Google, lets you review your options under Settings > Privacy options. The code ships with Google's test identifiers until the publisher configures production identifiers. When advertising is active, Google may process device and ad-interaction data such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address (which may be used to infer approximate location), app information, and diagnostics. Google handles this data under its own policies and the applicable ad and consent settings.

YNEdit does not request location permission and does not need your precise location to edit videos.

3. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

YNEdit uses Google's Firebase Analytics to measure app usage, improve its features, and understand the countries or regions where it is used. Firebase may automatically collect technical and usage information such as app interactions, sessions, app version, language, operating system, device model, app instance identifiers, and IP address. Google may infer approximate geographic location, such as country or region, from the IP address. YNEdit does not request location permission or collect precise GPS location for these analytics.

YNEdit logs aggregate events for actions such as creating a project, importing media (only its general category: audio or visual), starting playback or an export, completing or failing an export, starting an audio recording, splitting or reversing a clip, changing Premium feature status, and opening tools such as text, filters, adjustments, effects, masks, transitions, and audio controls. Tool events use fixed feature names. Your media files, file paths, project names or identifiers, edited text, and the contents of your videos, images, or audio are not sent to Analytics.

Google processes this information as a Firebase service provider under its terms and privacy policies. Firebase project configuration determines retention periods. The current version of YNEdit does not provide an in-app setting to disable Analytics. Device or Google privacy settings may offer additional controls, depending on the device and region; uninstalling the app prevents future collection from it but does not automatically delete data already sent to Google.

4. Subscriptions and Billing

Premium subscriptions are purchased and managed through Google Play. YNEdit receives the purchase status and subscription product from Google Play to activate or remove Premium features. Payments, payment-method details, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies. YNEdit does not receive your full card details. We do not send purchase identifiers or billing tokens to Firebase Analytics.

5. Data Sharing

YNStudios does not sell your media files or project contents. Google may receive information needed to provide its advertising and analytics services. Providers may process information in other countries under their own privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety.

6. Retention and Deletion

YNEdit projects, settings, and temporary files remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Exported videos saved elsewhere must be deleted from those locations. Analytics data already sent is subject to Firebase configuration and retention periods; uninstalling YNEdit does not automatically delete it.

7. Children's Privacy

YNEdit is a general-purpose editing tool and is not specifically designed to collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has directly provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the app, its integrations, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with its update date.
